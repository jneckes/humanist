Three modes

One window, three ways to work

Read mode

Reading a deck

    Review mode

    Notes on the slide itself

    • Switch to Review with Cmd+2 on a Mac, or Ctrl+2 on Windows and Linux.
    • Drag a circle around anything on this slide, then type or dictate what should change.
    • Your notes are kept by Blueline, next to this file, until you send them on.

    A chart to practise on: circle the tallest bar and ask for a label.

    Edit mode

    Click this headline and retype it

    • Switch to Edit with Cmd+3 on a Mac, or Ctrl+3 elsewhere.
    • Click a heading or a sentence, change the words, and press Enter. The file is saved at once.
    • Press Esc instead to leave the text as it was.

    Files from other people

    Protected view keeps you safe

    Protected

    A file that came from the internet opens with network and editing off, and a yellow bar says so. Allow network or Enable editing when you trust it.

    Trusted

    A file you made opens trusted: its scripts and fonts load, and you can edit it. Every document still runs sealed off from your other files.

    Working with Claude

    When the file changes, the view follows

    If Claude or another app rewrites this file, Blueline reloads it in place and keeps your slide, your scroll position and an open note. While you are typing, it waits until you finish.
