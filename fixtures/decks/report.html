Summary

New accounts that reach their first shared dashboard within seven days renew at almost twice the rate of those that do not.

Most delays happen before the product is even opened: security review, single sign-on and data access approvals.

  • Median time to first dashboard: 11 days
  • Accounts that stalled on SSO: 9 of 24
  • Accounts with a named internal champion: 15 of 24

Method

We interviewed admins and first users at 24 accounts that signed between April and July, and matched their answers to product telemetry.

Who we talked to

  1. Twelve mid-market accounts (100–999 employees)
  2. Eight enterprise accounts (1,000+ employees)
  3. Four accounts that churned in their first quarter

What we measured

Telemetry came from the onboarding_events table, joined on account id.

Findings

Approvals, not product, set the pace

Accounts that started security review before signing reached their first dashboard six days sooner.

We bought it in June and our analysts first logged in at the end of July.

Champions matter more than seats

Of the four churned accounts, none had a named champion after the kickoff call.

Recommendations

  1. Send the security pack with the order form, not after it.
  2. Offer a thirty-minute SSO session in week one.
  3. Ask every account to name a champion before kickoff.

Together these should bring the median time to first dashboard under seven days.
